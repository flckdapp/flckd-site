You're Flocked
==============

Source: https://github.com/flckdapp/flckd
Beta (TestFlight): https://testflight.apple.com/join/PmRqmMvb

SHORT DESCRIPTION
You're Flocked is an iPhone app that shows nearby surveillance cameras, warns you as you approach, and plans routes that pass fewer of them. Camera locations come from community-contributed map data. Route planning runs on the phone.

KEY FACTS
- Appears as FLCKD on the iPhone home screen, where the full name does not fit.
- iPhone, iOS 17 or later, currently a public beta installed through TestFlight. Android is planned, not currently available.
- Camera map, proximity alerts, configurable camera filters and alert distances.
- On-device camera-avoidance routing using downloadable region packs.
- No accounts, analytics, telemetry or stored location history in the app.
- Inspired by DeFlock; uses DeFlock and OpenStreetMap camera data.
- Open source at the repository above; self-hostable routing-pack builder and tile endpoint.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT
Camera coverage depends on community contributions. You're Flocked cannot guarantee that all cameras are mapped or that a route avoids surveillance. Routing works offline after a region is downloaded; fresh camera queries, place searches and Apple map imagery still use network services. Consult the full privacy document for what leaves the device:
https://github.com/flckdapp/flckd/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

You're Flocked is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Flock Safety.
The app is in public beta via the TestFlight link above. No App Store listing, price or launch date is asserted in this fact sheet.
